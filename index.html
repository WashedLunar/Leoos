LeoOS 0.4

A tiny hobby operating system, running in an x86 emulator in your browser

  • Tap to click. Drag with your finger to move windows or draw in Paint.
  • Tap Keyboard to type into the window you last tapped (Terminal, Notepad or Calculator).
  • New in 0.4: a Files app (folders, rename, delete, copy), a Browser for pages inside LeoOS, and three new games: 2048, Deep Dive and Tetris. The Terminal has mkdir, cd, neofetch and many more; type help.
  • New in 0.3: each window has a _ button to minimise it. Tap its button on the taskbar to bring it back. Drag the green corner tab to resize Terminal, Paint, Notepad, Files or Browser.
  • Games: Snake (walls wrap; tap the arrows or the board), Minesweeper (Flag button for taps), 2048 (swipe or the on-screen pad), Deep Dive (hold where you want to swim) and Tetris (side pad, or arrow keys / WASD).
  • Notepad can Save and Open files, including in folders. Try ls docs and cat docs/welcome.txt in the Terminal. Files live in memory, so they're gone when you restart.
  • On a computer you can just use your mouse and keyboard. LeoOS boots in a few seconds.
